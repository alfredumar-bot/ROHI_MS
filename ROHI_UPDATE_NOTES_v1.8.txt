ROHI Attendance v1.8 - Pydroid Test

1. GPS reliability fix
- Borno State Office: 11.797352, 13.143040 (100 m)
- Adamawa HQ: 9.2781640, 12.432640 (100 m)
- Android LocationManager now uses:
  * live GPS/network callbacks
  * a polling fallback for Android/Pydroid builds that do not deliver callbacks
  * immediate recent last-known phone fix when accurate and recent
- The office coordinate is NEVER used as the phone's current coordinate.
- GPS fix must be within the registered office geofence before Check-In.

2. Manual send buttons after generating reports
- Attendance Reports: SEND TO GOOGLE EXCEL
- Timesheet: SEND TIMESHEET TO GOOGLE EXCEL
- Leave Management: SEND LEAVE MANAGEMENT TO GOOGLE EXCEL
- These send buttons use the configured upload endpoint.
- Existing 3-minute auto-sync remains active.

3. Registration
- Registration submission immediately generates the staff Excel template and queues it for upload.
- Existing 3-minute staff auto-sync remains active.

4. Google Drive upload
- Google Drive/Google Sheets share URLs are browse links and cannot receive an XLSX POST directly.
- google_drive_upload.gs is included as a ready Google Apps Script upload receiver.
- Set attendance/staff folder IDs in the script, deploy it as a Web App, then paste its /exec URL into the matching upload endpoint fields in Server Connection.
- Timesheet and Leave folder IDs from the supplied link workbook are already filled in.

5. Auto-sync
- Attendance: every 3 minutes
- Timesheet: every 3 minutes
- Leave: every 3 minutes
- Staff Registration: every 3 minutes
- Immediate registration export/upload remains enabled.
